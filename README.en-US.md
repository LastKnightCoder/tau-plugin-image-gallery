# Gallery

Insert an image gallery into your document to group multiple images together with three layouts:

- **Row**: Arranges images into equal-height rows by aspect ratio—great for photography and screenshot sets.
- **Masonry**: Stacks images across columns—ideal for portrait-heavy collections.
- **Carousel**: Displays one image at a time with left/right navigation.

## How to Use

1. Type `/` on an empty line and select **Gallery** to insert.
2. Click **Add images to gallery** to upload files, paste images from the clipboard, or enter image URLs; drag images to reorder them.
3. Click a gallery to use its top property toolbar to switch layouts, **Edit images**, or open **Full-screen preview**. While selected, press `Enter` to edit, `Space` to preview, or `Delete` to remove.

Corner radius and shadow follow the image appearance in **Settings → Appearance** by default, and can be overridden per gallery in the edit dialog. Exporting to Markdown outputs one image link per line; exporting to HTML, EPUB, or copying to WeChat Official Account renders a horizontally scrollable image strip.
